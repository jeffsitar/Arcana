⚔️ LOCAL 2-PLAYER SETUP

📜 HOW TO PLAY

  1. Dealer Reveals (1–9): A random card from the Contester's dedicated 1–9 dealer deck is flipped face-up.
  2. Contester Picks Secretly (0–10): The Contester chooses one card from their remaining hand and locks it face-down.
  3. Bettor Predicts & Wagers: Without seeing the card, the Bettor predicts BIG (Contester > Dealer) or SMALL (Contester < Dealer) and wagers chips.
  4. Showdown & Role Swap: The hidden card flips! Scores are awarded separately, used cards are permanently removed, and roles swap for the next round.

⚖️ OFFICIAL SCORING RULES

  • Starting Gear: 11 cards (0–10) and 10 betting chips each. 18 rounds total.
  • Contester Reward: If Contester's card > Dealer's card, Contester gains (Contester Card − Dealer Card) chips.
  • Bettor Payout: Correct BIG/SMALL prediction wins +Wager chips. Incorrect prediction loses −Wager chips.
  • Exact Draw: If Contester Card = Dealer Card, nobody gains chips and the Bettor's wager is returned.
  • Strict Separation: Contester rewards and Bettor payouts are calculated independently and never double-counted.
  • Victory: Match ends after 18 rounds or immediately if either player hits 0 chips. Highest chip balance wins!
